NyayaSetu

AI-driven judicial operations platform

Undertrial prisoners in India can wait years for a hearing because their case falls through the cracks. NyayaSetu helps court and prison staff see which cases need attention, with a 0–100 score made from six factors anyone can inspect.

  • Tesseract OCR on FIRs and charge sheets, with semantic search in Qdrant
  • Case Q&A grounded in source documents, with [S1] [S2] citations
  • 10 analytics views with CSV/PDF export, role-based access and an audit log

Endless runner with no canvas, no engine and no audio files

  • Three levels with drones, lasers, shields and an overclock power-up
  • Upgrades and achievements saved between runs
